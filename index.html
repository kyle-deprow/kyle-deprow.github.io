How I work

Models are one part of the system.

I’m an engineer and technical leader who likes the full path from a rough research idea to software people can use. That means deciding where a model helps, where deterministic logic is safer, and how the whole product behaves under real constraints.

I started in mechanical engineering and robotics, moved through autonomy and applied machine learning, and now build health and agriculture products. I’m happiest when the work crosses perception, data, control, and product engineering.

Selected public work

Experiments, integrations, and engineering playgrounds.

A few examples of the side work that keeps me learning.

Prototype · real-time translation

Palancar ↗

A TypeScript prototype for Even G2 smart glasses. A G2 client streams speech to an authenticated Azure relay, which checks the language and returns English translations with suggested replies. Spanish and Turkish are the initial target languages.

Integration · local AI interface

G2 OpenClaw ↗

Connects Even G2 smart glasses to the upstream OpenClaw assistant through a Python WebSocket gateway with faster-whisper and a TypeScript/BLE client. Speech comes in through the glasses and streamed responses return to their display.

Experiment scaffold · bioinformatics

CAFA 6 Protein Function ↗

Python loaders, competition-metric evaluation, and submission validation for assigning Gene Ontology terms from amino-acid sequences in the CAFA 6 competition.

Earlier project · quantitative ML

QuantiPy ↗

A TensorFlow and pandas framework for defining features, prediction targets, and LSTM architectures for quantitative analysis. It grew from a Quantopian experiment and remains an early quantitative-ML playground.
